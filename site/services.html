Our services

Practical help for small businesses, startups, community projects and social enterprises in Newcastle and the North East.

Friendly, practical advice for small businesses and community enterprises, from shaping an idea and choosing a legal structure to planning, finance basics and finding funding.

Sessions that build the skills you need to start, run and grow an organisation with a social purpose.

Opportunities to meet other founders, community organisers, partners and funders, share ideas and work together.
