Coworking Thursdays

Free hot desking for startups, community projects and social enterprises, every Thursday from 10am to 4pm at Divercity Hub in Newcastle.

The details

  • WhenEvery Thursday, 10am to 4pm. First session Thursday 19 November 2026.
  • WhereDivercity Hub, [Divercity Hub street address and postcode], Newcastle upon Tyne
  • CostFree
  • Places20 desks each week

The space is provided free of charge by our partner, Divercity Hub, until CDCE opens its own building.

How to book

  1. Book your free place for the Thursday you want to come.
  2. Arrive any time between 10am and 4pm.
  3. Sign in when you arrive and find a desk.

If a week is full, we'll add you to the waiting list and let you know if a place comes up.

Book a free desk

Who it's for

Is it for me?

Coworking Thursdays is for anyone working on something that benefits people and communities:

  • Startups
  • Community projects
  • Social enterprises

Not sure if you fit? Ask us. We'd rather say yes.

While you're here

A few simple rules

  • Be friendly and respectful to everyone
  • Keep calls and noise to a reasonable level
  • Leave your desk as you found it
  • Let us know if you can't come, so someone else can have your place

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Divercity Hub provides the space at no cost, so we can offer it free to you.

Do I need to book?

Yes, please. There are 20 places each week, and booking means we can keep a desk for you.

Can I come every week?

Of course. Just book each week you plan to come.

I have access needs. Can you help?

Please email hello@cdce.org.uk before you visit and we'll do our best to make sure the space works for you.
