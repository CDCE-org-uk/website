Free space to work on the things that make a difference

The Centre for Diversity, Community & Enterprise (CDCE) supports startups, community projects and social enterprises. We're starting with Coworking Thursdays: free hot desking every Thursday, 10am to 4pm, at Divercity Hub in Newcastle.

Coworking Thursdays

Everything you need to know

When

Every Thursday, 10am to 4pm. Come for an hour or stay all day.

Where

Divercity Hub, Newcastle.

Cost

Free. No membership, no catch.

Places

20 desks a week, so please book ahead.

More about Coworking Thursdays

Who it's for

Built for people starting something good

If you're building a business, running a community project or growing a social enterprise, you're welcome. Bring your laptop, meet people doing similar work, and get things done.

  • Startups
  • Community projects
  • Social enterprises

What's coming next

Coworking Thursdays is our first step. Next, we're planning training, events and practical support, and a dedicated CDCE building in 2027.
