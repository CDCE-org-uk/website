Volunteer with us

Give a few hours, meet great people and help startups, community projects and social enterprises in Newcastle thrive.

Ways to help

No special experience needed. Just a friendly attitude and a few hours to spare.

Coworking Thursdays

Welcome people, help with sign-in, set up and tidy the space, and make sure everyone feels at home. Come for a morning, an afternoon or the whole day.

Events

Help plan and run workshops, networking sessions and community events, from setting up the room to greeting guests.

Lead volunteer roles

Have experience in marketing, events, running a space, or finance and admin? Take a lead role, shape how CDCE grows and lead a small team of volunteers.

Why volunteer

What you get

  • Experience you can put on your CV
  • Connections with founders, community organisers and local organisations
  • Flexible hours that fit around your life
  • The chance to make a real difference in Newcastle

How to get involved

  1. Email us and tell us a little about yourself and how you'd like to help.
  2. We'll invite you for an informal chat, usually on a Thursday at DiverCity Hub.
  3. Agree when you'd like to help, and we'll show you the ropes.
