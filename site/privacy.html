Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Who we are

Centre for Diversity, Community & Enterprise ("CDCE", "we", "us") is a not-for-profit community organisation and is responsible for the personal information described here. You can contact us about your data at hello@cdce.org.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Bookings for Coworking Thursdays: your name, email address, organisation and the type of work you do, so we can manage places and contact you about your booking.
  • Signing in on the day: your name and the date, so we know who is in the building and can report how many people we support.
  • Emails: your name, email address and anything you include, so we can reply.

We only send you news about CDCE if you have said you'd like it. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use trusted services to run CDCE, such as Google for email and forms. We may share anonymous numbers, such as how many people attended, with partners and funders.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it, and review it at least once a year.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by emailing hello@cdce.org.uk. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
