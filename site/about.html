About CDCE

We are a not-for-profit community organisation in Newcastle, creating space, support and opportunity for the people and organisations that make our communities stronger.

Why we exist

Good ideas need a place to grow

Across the North East, people are starting businesses, running community projects and building social enterprises, often from kitchen tables and borrowed rooms. Finding space, support and the right people can be hard.

CDCE was set up to change that. We run free coworking every Thursday, offer advice, guidance, training and events, and are developing a dedicated CDCE building in Newcastle.

Our mission

To provide space, support and opportunity that help community organisations and enterprises thrive, and open up opportunity for local people.

Our name is our purpose

Diversity, community, enterprise

Diversity
